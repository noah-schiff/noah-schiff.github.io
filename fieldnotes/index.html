Connect ArcGIS Online

One-time setup, about 3 minutes. Do it in ArcGIS Online on a computer or in Safari.

  1. Sign in at arcgis.com with your school or work account and open Content.
  2. Choose New item → Developer credentials → OAuth credentials.
  3. For Redirect URLs, add this address exactly:
  4. Give it a title like “Fieldnotes”, finish, then copy its Client ID and paste it here.

Next you’ll sign in with ArcGIS. On first sync Fieldnotes creates a “Fieldnotes” layer in your content, and syncing adds your located notes as points with photos and memos attached.

Connect iNaturalist

  1. In Safari, sign in at inaturalist.org, then open inaturalist.org/users/api_token.
  2. Select all of the text on that page, copy it, and paste it below.

The token lasts 24 hours, so on days you post you’ll paste a fresh one. It stays on this phone and is only sent to iNaturalist.
